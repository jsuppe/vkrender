Presenters

Hide keeps a presenter connected but off screen. Kick disconnects them at the transport - their app must reconnect. Pin holds a presenter on screen through the rotation; Spotlight makes them the only tile. Audio controls will appear here once the pipeline carries audio - today it is video-only by design.

Displays

Present puts the scene on that monitor as a borderless window. With several monitors presenting, the scene spans them as one canvas (aspect-fit); each card previews exactly that display's slice. The camera keeps broadcasting the full composite either way.
